Privacy Policy — MuseCat Auto Share

Last Updated: November 20, 2026  |  ← Back to Home

1. Overview

MuseCat Auto Share is a local Windows desktop application designed to help content creators organize and upload their own gameplay video clips to YouTube, Google Drive, Instagram, and TikTok using official platform APIs.

2. Data We Collect and Where It Is Stored

MuseCat Auto Share operates 100% locally on your computer. The developer does not operate any remote database, analytics server, or cloud storage that collects your personal data.

  • OAuth Tokens & API Credentials: Stored strictly on your local machine inside your local application data folder (data/credentials/ or %LOCALAPPDATA%\MuseCatAutoShare\data) solely to authenticate your uploads directly with Google, Meta, and TikTok.
  • Video Clips & Metadata: Read directly from the local folder you explicitly select (e.g., your OBS Studio / Aitum Vertical folder) and uploaded directly from your PC to the platforms you choose.
  • Local Queue Database: A local SQLite file stores your clip titles, descriptions, hashtags, and upload status history on your own device.

3. Third-Party Platform APIs & Limited Use

When you connect your accounts, MuseCat Auto Share communicates directly with the following official APIs on your behalf:

  • Google & YouTube API Services: Used strictly to upload user-selected videos to YouTube (youtube.upload) and back up user-selected clips to Google Drive (drive.file). MuseCat Auto Share's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. See also the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Meta / Instagram Graph API: Used strictly to publish user-selected Reels to the connected Instagram Professional account.
  • TikTok Content Posting API: Used strictly to upload user-selected videos to the authorized TikTok account's Inbox/Drafts or profile.

4. Data Sharing & Selling

We never sell, rent, share, or transfer your personal data, OAuth tokens, or video content to any third party or advertising network.

5. Revoking Access & Deleting Your Data

You remain in full control of your data at all times:

  • To delete all local data and tokens, simply delete the local data/ folder or uninstall MuseCat Auto Share.
  • You can revoke API access at any time from your Google Account Permissions, Instagram Apps and Websites settings, or TikTok Manage app permissions settings.
