100% Free, Stream-Safe Windows desktop tool for Kick, Twitch & YouTube streamers. Automatically watches your OBS Studio + Aitum Vertical clips, backs them up to Google Drive, and publishes them to YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, and TikTok using official APIs.
